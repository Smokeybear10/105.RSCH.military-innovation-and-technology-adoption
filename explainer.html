Board of Ordnance & Fortification

About the archive

The Board of Ordnance and Fortification study brings selected proposal transcriptions, military budgets, and research allotments into one searchable archive. The records cover different years and answer different questions.

Use the dashboard to find a subject, compare a period, or follow a recorded decision. Open the original action text before drawing conclusions from a category or chart.

What is included

The archive is a selected collection, not a complete inventory of the Board’s activity. The separate researcher charts may use different source sets and periods.

Reading the records

Proposal categories are automated

Keyword rules group the recorded action as Approved, Investigating, Rejected, or Other. Missing or unmatched actions belong to Other. These labels help navigation; they are not independently verified historical findings.

An approval category can include authorization or funding, not necessarily adoption by the Army. Technology clusters and government/private proposer labels are also inferred from text. Read the subject, proposer, and action together when assessing an entry.

Unknown dates remain unknown

Undated proposals stay available in the record list. The year chart excludes them. A blank technology-timeline cell means no entry in this dataset for that period, not proof that no work occurred.

Appropriations and allotments are different

Military budgets show Army and Navy appropriations. Board appropriations show funds assigned to the Board. Allotments record allocations to individual projects. They do not establish how much was ultimately spent, and a revocation is not a separate negative expenditure.

Year and allotment date are shown as transcribed and may differ; dates have not been reconciled against the original reports.

Adjusted dollar values come from the worksheet

The budget export includes nominal and 2025-dollar columns. The source worksheet supplies the adjusted values; its calculation method is not documented in this repository. The missing Army entry also makes the combined 1879 total incomplete.
