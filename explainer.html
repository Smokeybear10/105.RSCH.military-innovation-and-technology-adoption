Board of Ordnance & Fortification

Archive guide

This guide covers the Board of Ordnance and Fortification study: selected proposal transcriptions, appropriations, allotments, and technology reviews. Each collection has its own period and source coverage.

Use the archive to find a subject, compare periods, or follow a recorded decision. Read the transcribed action before drawing conclusions from a category or chart. These records support descriptive research; they do not establish why a technology was funded or adopted.

Reading the records

Proposal categories are automated

Keyword rules group the recorded action as Approved, Investigating, Rejected, or Other. Missing or unmatched actions belong to Other. These labels help navigation; they are not independently verified historical findings.

An approval category can include authorization or funding, not necessarily adoption by the Army. Technology clusters are inferred from subject keywords. Proposer labels use only identity cues; ambiguous or missing identities remain Unknown. Read the subject, proposer, and action together when assessing an entry.

Unknown dates remain unknown

Undated proposals stay available in the record list. The year chart excludes them. A blank technology-timeline cell means no entry in this dataset for that period, not proof that no work occurred.

Appropriations and allotments are different

Military budgets show Army and Navy appropriations. Board appropriations show funds assigned to the Board. Allotments record allocations to individual projects. They do not establish how much was ultimately spent, and a revocation is not a separate negative expenditure.

Year and allotment date are shown as transcribed and may differ; dates have not been reconciled against the original reports.

Missing actions and amounts

Only 910 of 1,691 proposals have a recorded action. The interface separates the 781 missing actions from other recorded actions. Approval-label shares use recorded actions as the denominator and are unavailable for periods with no actions. An approval label is not proof of adoption. The action-coverage comparison shows the denominators and gaps; the summary CSV contains the underlying counts.

Three allotments have no recorded amount. They remain in the archive and exports, but do not become zero-dollar allocations. Totals and charts use known amounts; a notice identifies incomplete totals.

Adjusted dollar values come from the worksheet

The budget export includes nominal and 2025-dollar columns. The source worksheet supplies literal adjusted values with no formulas or documented calculation method. Charts default to nominal dollars. Budget rows preserve the workbook, sheet, row number, and recorded citations; citation links are not independently verified. Two 1865 entries were restored after correcting the importer’s header row. The missing Army entry also makes the combined 1879 total incomplete.
