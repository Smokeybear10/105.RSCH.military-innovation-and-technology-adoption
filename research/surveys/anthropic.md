# Anthropic survey work

Status: the separate survey-test instrument and instructions are still needed. No survey test, respondent recruitment, private-data analysis, or instrument edits have been completed.

The public paper [The AI expectations gap](https://www.anthropic.com/research/ai-expectations-gap), published October 16, 2026, compares US adults with Anthropic staff. Its connection to the separate survey-test task has not been established. It is useful background, not a substitute for that task's instructions.

The [methods appendix](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/1bc1a3067f9c9883d6d556b38f26fd4f1e1b640f/The%20AI%20Expectations%20Gap%20-%20Appendix.pdf) distinguishes public fieldwork on August 11–23 from staff fieldwork on August 9–12. It also distinguishes unique respondents from completed interviews and documents differences in missing-response options and randomized question horizons. Those distinctions belong in any comparison table.

Once the test instrument is available, first establish whether the requested work concerns wording, survey flow, response coding, or quantitative validation. A useful first pass would check that response options and skip logic match the intended measures, that random assignment produces the intended groups, and that an export preserves missing values and question exposure. No test responses should enter a live research sample unless the project specifies that workflow.

A possible quantitative extension is a sensitivity analysis of how response scales, exclusions, or horizon assignment affect a published group comparison. That requires suitable data and an agreed question; it has not been performed.
