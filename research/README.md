The original Precise Mass records were collected by the research team. The work recorded here covers auditing, reference repairs, validation maintenance, and source review; it does not claim authorship of those original records. Other Board research contributions retain their attribution in the main README and source files.

Private working spreadsheets, collaborator comments, assignment documents, Slack conversations, and meeting-preparation scripts are not part of this public archive. The research notes describe the work and link to public evidence. Original files remain in the local working archive. Public-source links do not establish a right to redistribute the linked publications, so the bibliography links to publishers rather than bundling their PDFs.
