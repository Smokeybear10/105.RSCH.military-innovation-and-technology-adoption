Thomas Ou · Review completed 6 November 2026 · Public archive prepared 7 November 2026

This review covers 13 existing country-system records and 64 proposed edits or coding decisions. It reviews existing work; it does not claim authorship of the original records. The proposals had not been applied to the shared dataset as of the review date. They include evidence gaps and methodological choices, so 64 is not a count of proven factual errors.

The original review contained 77 source entries. This archive includes 76 public source entries; one internal coding-instructions source is omitted. Original workbook cells, collaborator entries and private document links are also omitted.

The archive preserves dated findings rather than asserting that every specification or delivery status remains current. Manufacturer claims, weak sources and partial access are identified in the source notes.

## Recurring decisions

- Separate orders, first receipt, testing and operational fielding.
- Match capabilities to the country, model, delivered configuration and observation date.
- Distinguish unverified capability from verified absence.
- Label radio, target, engagement and flight ranges explicitly.
- Treat reported contract prices, package totals and generic unit estimates separately.
- Date production rates; annualization is not measured annual output.
- Define whether commercial products include subsidiaries and historical offerings.
