## Five focused source checks

### United Kingdom — Nyan One-Way Effector

Corrected the unit-price entry from USD 132 million to the published estimate of approximately USD 132,000. The source uses approximately GBP 100,000 and supplies its own dollar conversion. The estimate is not a verified procurement unit price: the public award notice supplies a total without an aircraft count or a clear equipment allocation. Cleared the unsupported positive AI entry; pre-programmed flight alone does not establish AI or machine learning.

Still unresolved: the configuration and equipment included in the price, and exact-model evidence of AI use. Sources: [reported estimate](https://newatlas.com/military/royal-navy-launches-combat-drone-shipborne-catapult/), [Royal Navy announcement](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2026/july/03/20260703-strike-drone-launches-from-rn-ship), [award notice](https://www.sell2wales.gov.wales/search/show/search_view.aspx?ID=FEB589625&catID=).

### Taiwan — Switchblade 300

Entered 2026 as the best-supported receipt-year estimate and cleared 2024 as the operational-fielding year. The 2024 announcement concerns sale approval. Government testimony on 30 March 2026 said all 685 systems remained outstanding; an October report described receipt as completed in 2026. Earlier reporting claimed some arrived in 2025, so the conflicting chronology remains explicit. The sources do not date the first operational use.

Sources: [2024 announcement](https://www.avinc.com/2024/07/02/taiwan-to-receive-avs-switchblade-300-loitering-munition-systems/), [March testimony](https://ivod.ly.gov.tw/Demand/Speech/168036), [October receipt report](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aipl/202610240124.aspx).

### China — XS101

Cleared 2023 as the first-fielded year because the checked evidence describes testing. A general discussion of AI in loitering munitions did not establish an exact-model capability. The existing negative AI classification remained provisional pending a consistent unknown-value rule; it is not a finding that the system lacks AI.

The unresolved questions are first operational fielding and treatment of unconfirmed capability. This archive preserves the maintenance decision without republishing the internal coding instructions or presenting missing evidence as a verified negative.

### Taiwan — Albatross II

Changed the AI field to Yes using NCSIST’s exact-model description of AI-assisted recognition. The finding concerns that stated recognition capability; it does not establish full autonomy, a particular delivered configuration or operational acceptance. The first-fielding date remained blank.

Sources: [NCSIST English page](https://www.ncsist.org.tw/eng/csistdup/products/product.aspx?catalog=55&product_id=589), [NCSIST Chinese page](https://www.ncsist.org.tw/csistdup/products/product.aspx?catalog=56&product_id=606).

### China — CASC AR-2

Changed import status from Yes to No for the Chinese-produced system and cleared 2021 from the Chinese receipt/import and first-fielding fields. The 2021 delivery evidence concerns Indonesia receiving a Chinese product. It supports an export, not China importing or first fielding the system. The export evidence was retained.

Whether China’s own military fielded AR-2 remains unresolved. Domestic development and foreign sales do not establish domestic military use. Sources: [Chinese development report](https://military.people.com.cn/n1/2018/1108/c1011-30389592.html), [Indonesian delivery report](https://www.janes.com/defence-intelligence-insights/defence-news/indonesia-receives-first-batch-of-chinese-made-ar-2-missiles-for-its-ch-4-uavs).
