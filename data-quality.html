The archive contains selected transcriptions and derived categories. This page preserves review notes from May 12, 2026, with clarifications for the current public exports. References to private workbooks and older generated files remain for context; they do not imply that those files are included here.

Reading-room dates are parsed from research documents. Report ranges can be inconsistent, and biographical records can include dates extracted from citations or headings. Check the cited sources before treating these as verified chronologies. Report text includes both research summaries and excerpts.

Known gaps in the proposal corpus

The proposal records come from nine annual "Subjects Considered" tables transcribed from BOF annual reports. Several reports have structural gaps in the original transcriptions.

Missing recorded actions

781 of 1,691 rows · 46% of corpus · grouped as Other

The public export has no recorded action for 780 rows from the 1903–04 through 1907–08 source files, plus one row from 1897–98. These records do not establish the Board's decision.

Missing actions are grouped as Other in the current export and dashboard. Other also includes non-empty actions that did not match a rule. Neither group should be interpreted as a rejection or an approval.

BOF Annual Report number typo in FY1907–08

should read AR #20, file says AR #10

Every row in the 1907–08 source spreadsheet has BOF.AR.10.1907-1908 in the report-number column. Annual Report #10 corresponds to FY1899–1900; the correct value for 1907–08 is AR #20. This is a transcription typo in the upstream Excel file, not the pipeline.

File to fix · column "BOF Annual Report #", replace "AR.10" → "AR.20"

Undated records from the 1905–06 source file

133 records without a year in the public export

The 1905–06 source rows have BOF.AR.16 without a year suffix. All 133 remain undated in the current public export. They are available in the record list when undated records are included, but are excluded from the year chart. Assigning dates would require checking the source reports.

Known quirks in the budget ledger

Appropriations file extends one year past its filename

master_appropriations_1888_1919.csv ends in 1920

The 1920 row records the Board's dissolution: a zero-dollar entry noting that $230,000 of unspent prior appropriations was returned to the Treasury. It's kept as a marker for the Board's wind-down rather than dropped.

Files involved · decision: rename file to "1888_1920" or drop the 1920 row

  • Data/Appropriations/Completed Spreadsheets/*.xlsx "Statement of Appropriations Mad" sheet, trailing row

Defense budget series starts at 1866, not 1865

budget_master_ledger.csv

Headline copy refers to a 1865–1920 range; the underlying ledger's earliest data point is FY1866 (Army) / FY1866 (Navy). The 1865 fiscal year has no row because the original Statistical Abstract entry for that year is missing.

File to amend · add a 1865 row or rename to "1866-1920"

Allotments file ends at 1918

master_allotments_1888_1919.csv

The Board functionally stopped issuing new project allotments after 1918. Filename retains the 1919 boundary for symmetry with the appropriations file; no rows exist for 1919.
